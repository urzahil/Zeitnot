How this works

Inside this page is a chess engine written from scratch for it: a 0x88 board, full legal move generation (castling, en passant, promotions, checked against standard perft counts), and alpha-beta search with quiescence, check extensions and killer-move ordering. No libraries and no server.

It runs on several of your CPU cores at once as Web Workers. Each worker plays quick, slightly sloppy games against itself and checks every position: is material level, does no simple capture win, and is there exactly one move that wins at least three pawns or mates? Positions that pass become puzzles. That rules out "take the hanging piece", so what's left are forks, discovered attacks, sacrifices and mates.

A starter bank of puzzles, mined the same way and checked one ply deeper, lets you play straight away. Fresh ones from your own device (tagged ⚡) go to the front of the queue as soon as they're found.
